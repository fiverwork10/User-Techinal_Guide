## 1. Getting Started

### 🌐 Opening the website

- Open your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
- Go to: **http://localhost:3000**
- You will see the **Fast-FoodsX homepage** with the animated intro, trending dishes, and the full menu below.

> If the site doesn't load, make sure the computer has the **backend server** running too (see the Technical Guide for setup steps).

### 👤 Creating an account

1. Click **"Sign Up / Register"** in the top menu.
2. Fill in your **name**, **email**, **phone (optional)**, and a **password** (at least 6 characters).
3. Type the password again to confirm.
4. Click **Register** — you are now logged in automatically! 🎉

### 🔑 Logging in

1. Click **"Login"** in the top menu.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.
   - Regular customers go to the **homepage**.
   - Admin accounts go to the **Admin Panel**.

### 🔐 Forgot your password?

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password"**.
2. Enter your **email** and click **Send OTP**.
3. Check your email inbox for a **6-digit code**.
4. Enter the code, choose a **new password**, and click **Reset**.
5. Log in with your new password.

## 2. For Customers — How to Order Food

### 🍽️ Browsing the menu

- Click **"Dishes / Menu"** in the top menu to see all food items.
- Use the **search box** to find a dish by name.
- Use the **filters** to narrow down by:
  - **Category** (Pizza, Burgers, Desserts...)
  - **Price range**
  - **Availability**
  - **Rating**
- Use **Sort** to order by price (low→high, high→low), rating, or newest.
- Click **"Load More"** at the bottom to see more dishes.

### 📖 Viewing a dish

Click on any dish to open its **detail page**, where you can see:
- Pictures of the dish
- Full description
- **Sizes** (if available) — pick a size to change the price
- **Spice level** and **toppings** (if available)
- **Quantity** (+ / − buttons)
- **Special instructions** box (e.g., "no onions, extra cheese")
- **Rating** and customer **reviews**

### 🛒 Adding to cart

1. On a dish card or dish page, click **"Add to Cart"**.
2. The cart icon in the top menu shows the total number of items.
3. Click the **cart icon** to open your shopping cart.

### 🛍️ Your shopping cart

In the cart page you can:
- **Change quantities** with the + / − buttons
- **Remove** an item
- **Clear the whole cart**
- See a summary: **Subtotal**, **Delivery Fee**, **Tax**, and **Total**

> 💡 **Good to know:** Delivery is **free for orders above $500**. Otherwise a delivery fee of **$40** applies.

### 💳 Checkout (paying for your order)

1. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Contact details** (name, email, phone)
   - **Delivery address** — or click **"Use My Current Location"** to fill it automatically
3. Choose a **payment method**:
   - **Cash on Delivery** 💵 — pay when your food arrives
   - **Card** 💳 — enter card number, expiry, and CVV
   - **UPI** 📱 — enter your UPI ID
4. Add a **coupon code** if you have one (see the Coupons section below).
5. Add any **special instructions**.
6. Click **Place Order**.
7. Watch the **success screen** with your order number. 🎉

## 4. Coins, Vouchers & Rewards

### 🪙 What are Coins?

Coins are a **loyalty reward**. You earn coins and then spend them to get discounts.

**How to earn coins:**
- 🛒 **Place orders** — earn **1 coin for every $100** you spend
- ✨ **Tap "Collect"** on dish cards to earn **bonus coins**
- 🎁 **Special promotions** (birthday bonuses, referrals)

**How to use coins:**
- Look for dishes with a **coin discount** badge.
- Click **"Use Coins"** to apply your coins and get a discount on that dish.

### 🎟️ What are Vouchers?

Vouchers are special offers like **Free Delivery** or **% off** your order.

- You **earn a free-delivery voucher** for every **20 completed orders**.
- You can also **collect vouchers** by tapping "Collect" on special dishes.
- In **Coins & Vouchers**, click **"Use Now"** on a voucher, then place your order and the discount is applied at checkout.

> ⚠️ Vouchers have an **expiry date** — use them before they expire!

## 5. Order Tracking & Delivery

### 🚚 After you place an order

Your order goes through these statuses:

```
Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered
```

If the restaurant cannot fulfill it, the order may be marked **Cancelled / Rejected** (with a reason).

### 📍 Tracking live

- Open **Dashboard → Order Tracking**.
- The status updates **live** — no need to refresh the page.
- You can also **chat with the restaurant** if you have questions about your order.

## 6. Reviews & Ratings

### ⭐ Writing a review

1. Open a dish's detail page and scroll to **Customer Reviews**.
2. Choose a **star rating** (1 to 5).
3. Type your **comment** (and add an emoji 😋 or a **photo** if you like).
4. Click **Submit**.

### 🔍 A note about approval

Reviews are checked by the restaurant before they appear publicly. **Only approved reviews are shown** to everyone — so if your review doesn't appear immediately, it's waiting for approval.

## 7. Support Chat & Contact

### 💬 Live chat

- Open the **chat window** from your dashboard (or the chat icon).

## 8. For Admins — Running the Store

> To access the admin panel: log in with an **admin account**, then go to **http://localhost:3000/admin**

### 🛡️ First-time admin setup

If no admin account exists yet:
1. Go to **/admin/register**.
2. Create the admin account (name, email, password).
3. Log in with those details.

> The default admin created by the setup script is `admin@fastfoodsx.com` / `Admin@123`.

### 🎛️ The admin dashboard

| Menu item | What it lets you do |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Dashboard** | Overview: dishes, categories, customers, orders, revenue, average rating + live charts & activity feed |
| **Manage Dishes** | Add, edit, delete, search, filter, and export (CSV) food items; upload images; set sizes, spice, toppings |
| **Manage Categories** | Add, edit, delete, and reorder (drag & drop) menu categories |
| **Manage Orders** | See all orders, change status, search, bulk delete, export CSV, chat with customers |
| **Delivery Management** | See deliveries, assign delivery partners, track status, view charts |
| **Customer Management** | View all customers and their details |
| **Reviews** | Approve, reject, reply to, or delete customer reviews |
| **Coupons & Vouchers** | Create coupons, coin discounts, and vouchers; give bonus coins |
| **Payments** | View payment records, approve/decline, export |
| **Notifications** | View all notifications, mark read/delete |
| **System Settings** | Store details, payment options, delivery fees, tax, working hours, and more |
| **Admin Chat** | Reply to customer support messages in real time |

### ⚙️ Useful admin tips

- **To change the delivery fee or tax:** go to **System Settings → Delivery / Tax**.
- **To make a dish available/unavailable:** edit the dish and toggle **Available**.
- **To add a deal:** go to **Deals** in the admin panel, create a deal, pick dishes, and set the discount.
- **To approve a review:** go to **Reviews**, click **Approve**.

## 9. For Delivery Partners (Riders)

### 📝 Registering as a rider

1. Open the **Rider Registration** page (link in the footer/menu).
2. Fill in:
   - **Personal info** (name, email, phone, age)
   - **Address** (street, city, state, ZIP)
   - **Vehicle & license** (bike/scooter/car, vehicle number, license number)
   - A **password**
   - An optional **profile photo**
3. Click **Register as Rider**.
4. An admin reviews your application before you start delivering.

### 🛵 While delivering

- Admins assign deliveries to you from the **Delivery Management** page.
- Keep your **location** and **status** updated so customers can track their food.
- Your **rating**, **deliveries completed**, and **earnings** are tracked by the system.

## 10. Troubleshooting & FAQ

### ❓ "I can't log in"
- Make sure your email and password are correct (password is at least 6 characters).
- Try **Forgot Password** to reset it.
- If the message says **"Your account has been deactivated"**, contact the store admin.

### ❓ "The page says my review is pending"
- Reviews are approved by the admin before they're shown. This is normal.

### ❓ "My cart is empty after I refresh"
- Make sure you're **logged in** — the cart is saved per account.

### ❓ "The delivery fee seems high"
- Orders **above $500** get **free delivery**. Below that, a delivery fee applies.

### ❓ "Where is my voucher / coin?"
- Check **Dashboard → Coins & Vouchers**.
- Remember, coins are earned per order and vouchers have expiry dates.

### ❓ "The chat isn't sending"
- Make sure you're logged in, and that the internet/server is working. Try reloading the page.

### ❓ "What do the order statuses mean?"

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Your order was received, waiting to be confirmed |
| **Confirmed** | The restaurant accepted your order |
| **Preparing** | Your food is being cooked 🍳 |
| **Out for Delivery** | A rider is on the way 🛵 |
| **Delivered** | Enjoy your food! 🍔 |
| **Cancelled / Rejected** | Could not be fulfilled (reason shown) |
